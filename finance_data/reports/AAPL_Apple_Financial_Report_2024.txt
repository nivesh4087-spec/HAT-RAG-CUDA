# Apple Inc. (AAPL) - Annual Financial & Accounting Report (Form 10-K)
## Section 1: Executive Financial Performance and Income Statement Summary
Apple Inc. reported total consolidated net sales of $383,285 million ($383.28B) with gross profit of $169,148 million, representing an overall gross margin of 44.1%. Product net sales (iPhone, Mac, iPad, Wearables) generated $298,085 million, while Services revenue (App Store, Apple Music, iCloud, ApplePay) reached an all-time record of $85,200 million. Total operating expenses were $54,847 million, comprising $29,915 million in Research & Development (R&D) and $24,932 million in SG&A. Consolidated operating income stood at $114,301 million, yielding net income of $96,995 million ($6.13 diluted EPS).
## Section 2: Balance Sheet, Working Capital and Liquidity Disclosures
Total assets stood at $352,583 million. Current assets included $29,965 million in Cash and cash equivalents, $31,590 million in Marketable securities, $29,508 million in Accounts receivable, and $6,331 million in Inventories. Current liabilities totaled $145,308 million, including $62,610 million in Accounts payable and $7,995 million in Deferred revenue. Non-current liabilities included $95,281 million in Long-term term debt. Total shareholders equity stood at $62,146 million.
## Section 3: Critical Accounting Policies & Revenue Recognition (ASC 606)
Revenue is recognized when control of promised goods or services is transferred to customers. Under ASC 606, hardware sales with bundled software updates are accounted for as multiple performance obligations with transaction prices allocated on a relative standalone selling price (SSP) basis. R&D costs are expensed as incurred. Inventories are stated at the lower of cost or net realizable value (NRV) using the first-in, first-out (FIFO) method.
