# NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) - Annual Financial & Accounting Report (Form 10-K)
## Section 1: Financial Results and Hyper-Scale AI Data Center Revenue
NVIDIA reported total annual revenue of $60,922 million ($60.92B), representing a 126% annual expansion driven by enterprise generative AI deployment. Compute & Networking segment generated $47,405 million, propelled by NVIDIA Hopper H100 and HGX platform deployments. Graphics segment (GeForce gaming and professional visualization) contributed $13,517 million. Gross margin expanded to 72.7% ($44,301 million gross profit). Total operating expenses were $11,329 million (R&D of $8,675 million). Operating income surged to $32,972 million (54.1% operating margin), resulting in consolidated net income of $29,760 million ($11.93 diluted EPS).
## Section 2: Working Capital, Inventory Commitments and Balance Sheet
Total balance sheet assets reached $65,728 million, up from $41,182 million in the prior fiscal period. Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities totaled $25,984 million. Inventories were $5,282 million, consisting of raw materials, work-in-progress, and finished goods. Outstanding inventory purchase obligations and long-term supply agreements with semiconductor foundries (TSMC) totaled $16,100 million to secure wafer manufacturing capacity. Total stockholders equity expanded to $42,978 million.
## Section 3: Accounting for Semiconductor Production, Warrants and Taxes
Inventories are valued on a FIFO basis and written down to net realizable value for estimated excess and obsolete quantities based on demand forecasting. R&D expenditures for semiconductor integrated circuit masks and tape-outs are expensed as incurred until technological feasibility is proven. The effective tax rate was 12.0%, benefiting from foreign derived intangible income (FDII) deductions and federal research tax credits.
